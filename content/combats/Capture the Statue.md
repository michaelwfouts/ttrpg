# Capture the Statue

## Encounter Overview

- **Encounter Hook** - Claim more corporate-sponsored statues than the other team before the buzzer, all while the arena's own advertisements turn into arena hazards around you.

- **Setup/Context** - The bout is staged in the Coliseum of Port Nyanzaru, a venue run by an ultra-capitalist crowd that monetizes everything, including the traps. The statues, the lighting, and the mid-match "complications" are all sponsored ad gimmicks the crowd expects to see, so nothing that happens on the floor is ever purely cosmetic; it's also a commercial. Two teams compete for bragging rights and crowd favor rather than each other's lives.

- **Intro**

  > You step out onto the sun-bleached sand of the Coliseum floor to a wall of noise, drums, horns, and an announcer's voice booming your team's sponsor name over the crowd. Five golden statues gleam in the center of the arena, each etched with a different merchant house's crest. Above you, enormous banners flap advertising everything from spiced wine to enchanted sandals, and you notice the base of the nearest statue is ringed with dark, unlit fixtures, waiting for something.

  The DM should have the opposing team's entrance called out immediately after, giving both sides their first look at each other before the horn sounds.

- **Key Features**
  - **Quadrant control** - The battlefield is divided into 4 quadrants, with each team's home territory on opposite ends of the arena. Only two statues can occupy a single quadrant at once, so a team going for a 3-statue win must spread control across at least two quadrants, making the lead harder to simply turtle and defend.
  - **Moving a statue** - Carrying a statue halves a creature's speed for the turn. A creature can instead make a Strength check as part of its Interact with an Object action to attempt to move it at full speed that turn.
  - **Advertisement hazard** - At fixed intervals (every round or two) the sponsors cut in with a new "bit." Each hazard can be announced at the top of the round:

    | Hazard | Effect | Announcer Callout |
    |---|---|---|
    | Freezer advertisement | The frosted statues become slippery; any creature that ends its turn adjacent to one must succeed on a Dexterity save or fall prone | "Feeling the heat out there, folks? Frostbyte Ale is here to cool things down... in about sixty seconds!" The statues visibly begin frosting over with condensation as the crowd cheers. |
    | Rave advertisement | Each statue base blares music and strobes disco lights; any creature within 5 feet must make a Wisdom save or have its speed halved and attack rolls made at disadvantage until the start of its next turn, and other creatures have advantage on attack rolls against it | "Time to turn it UP! Midnight Elixir presents... the after-party!" House music swells and the lights along the statue bases begin to flicker to life in warning. |
    | Sandstorm cologne advertisement | A thick, perfumed haze billows out from vents around the arena's edge; the affected quadrant becomes heavily obscured, and any creature that starts its turn inside must succeed on a Constitution save or be blinded until the start of its next turn | "Smell like a champion! Dune Storm cologne, now with 100% more atmosphere!" Vents along the quadrant walls hiss and rattle as pressure builds inside them. |
    | Energy tonic advertisement | Crackling arcs of harmless-looking lightning leap between the statue bases; any creature that ends its turn within 10 feet of a statue must succeed on a Dexterity save or be pushed 10 feet toward the nearest quadrant boundary and take a jolt of thunder damage | "Feel the SURGE! Voltbrew Energy Tonic, official sponsor of tonight's main event!" Sparks begin to dance visibly along the statues' bases as the crowd chants along. |

- **Combatants**
  - **Sunblade Duelist** - *Custom, scale to party*
    - **Description** - The team's front-line showman, a flashy blade-for-hire who treats every clash like it's the main event.
    - **Special Abilities**
      - **Traits**
        - Showboater. Has advantage on the first attack roll it makes each combat.
      - **Actions**
        - Multiattack. Makes two Showboat Strike attacks.
        - Showboat Strike. A melee weapon attack; on a hit, the Duelist can forgo dealing damage to instead knock the target's held statue loose, forcing it to drop to the ground in its space.
        - Riposte (Reaction). When a creature misses it with a melee attack, makes one Showboat Strike attack against that creature.
  - **Sandtrap Trickster** - *Custom, scale to party*
    - **Description** - A quick, unarmored skirmisher who treats the arena's own hazards as tools rather than threats.
    - **Special Abilities**
      - **Traits**
        - Hazard Sense. Always aware of the location and next trigger round of any active or upcoming advertisement hazard.
      - **Actions**
        - Dust Toss. Forces one creature within 15 feet to make a Constitution save or be blinded until the end of its next turn.
        - Vanish into the Crowd (Bonus Action). Takes the Hide action, treating the roaring coliseum crowd along the arena's edge as sufficient cover.
  - **Crowd-Pleaser Battlemage** - *Custom, scale to party*
    - **Description** - A showy spellcaster who plays as much to the stands as to the fight itself.
    - **Special Abilities**
      - **Actions**
        - Spotlight Flare (Recharge 5-6). Forces every creature in a 15-foot cone to make a Dexterity save or be blinded until the start of its next turn.
        - Encore (Bonus Action). Grants one ally within 30 feet advantage on its next saving throw before the end of its turn.
        - Ad-lib Ward (Reaction). When an ally within 30 feet is hit by an attack, reduces the damage that ally takes.

- **Resolution**
  - **Victory** - At the end of round 5 (30 seconds), the team with the most statues held in their own territory wins the bout.
  - **Failure/alternate outcome** - A team that goes down to zero standing combatants before the horn automatically forfeits any statues currently in dispute; the other team is declared the winner regardless of the statue count at that moment.
  - **Aftermath** - The crowd roars for whichever team put on the better show, regardless of the final score, and the winning team's sponsor banner is raised over the arena as vendors immediately start hawking commemorative merchandise.
  - **Thematic rewards**
    - **Sponsor's Championship Sash** - A garish silk sash bearing the winning house's crest; wearing it in Port Nyanzaru grants advantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks made to haggle with that house's merchants for the rest of the campaign.
    - **Chilled Novelty Goblet** - Lifted from the freezer-ad promotional stand; keeps a drink ice cold for 1 hour once per day and makes a decent conversation piece.
    - **Miniature Disco Statue** - A palm-sized replica of one of the arena statues that flickers with the same disco lights on command; purely cosmetic, but the crowd loves it.
